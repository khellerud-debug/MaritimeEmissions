Financial Summary Definitions

Accrued Liability: The total current market value of the EUAs required to cover the emissions produced to date. It is your current, unrealized cost.
(Used Allowances x Current Market Price)
Value of Holdings: The total current market value of all the EUAs you currently own.
(Owned Allowances x Current Market Price)
Net Exposure: The difference between the value of your holdings and your accrued liability. A positive value indicates a surplus, while a negative value (in red) shows your immediate financial shortfall if you had to settle today.
(Value of Holdings - Accrued Liability)
Unrealized P&L: The "paper" profit or loss on your EUA holdings, based on the change between your average purchase price and the current market price.
((Current Price - Average Purchase Price) x Owned Allowances)

EEXI: A one-time certification of a vessel's technical design efficiency. It's the starting point.
SEEMP: The Ship Energy Efficiency Management Plan. A dynamic, onboard plan detailing how the vessel will operate efficiently to meet its targets.
CII: The Carbon Intensity Indicator. An annual operational rating (A-E) based on actual emissions and distance traveled. The SEEMP is the plan to achieve a good CII rating.
